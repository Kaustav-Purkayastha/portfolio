About Me

My Journey

I am a Data Engineer with 3+ years of experience architecting and delivering enterprise-grade data platforms in the Financial Services domain. Currently at Deloitte USI, I work across both a large-scale cloud data platform (Amazon Redshift) and an active migration to a Databricks medallion lakehouse — designing ETL/ELT pipelines, dimensional models, and dbt-based transformations for Fortune 500 insurance clients across the full data lifecycle, from source ingestion through regulatory reporting and downstream consumption.

I hold a Master of Computer Applications (Data Science & Finance) from Lovely Professional University and a Bachelor of Science (Hons.) in Computer Science from Gurucharan College, Silchar. My technical depth spans SQL, Python, PySpark, AWS (Redshift, S3, Glue), Databricks, Delta Lake, dbt, Snowflake, and Informatica IDMC, with applied knowledge in data modeling, SCD/CDC patterns, source-to-target reconciliation, data quality frameworks, and business analytics.

I am driven by the challenge of converting complex business logic into clean, scalable, production-grade data systems — whether re-platforming legacy ETL to modern ELT or building AI-assisted conversion and reconciliation frameworks. I actively explore AI-enabled engineering workflows using Amazon Bedrock and Anthropic Claude, and am focused on contributing to modern data platforms that deliver measurable business impact.
